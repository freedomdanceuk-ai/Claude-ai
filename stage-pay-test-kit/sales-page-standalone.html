The problem

You send the money. You get a photo. You hope.

01 · Money without a trail

Transfers go out. Nobody tracks what they bought.

After a year of sending money, most people can't say how much went into the walls and how much disappeared along the way.

02 · Proof that proves nothing

One blurry photo looks the same every week.

Without asking for specific angles, dates and receipts, you can't tell real progress from an old picture.

03 · Family makes it harder

Asking for proof feels like accusing someone.

When a brother, an uncle or a family friend is running the site, a fair rule set in advance keeps the relationship safe.

How it works

Three steps, repeated at every stage of the build.

1

Set the stage and the price

Enter your agreed amount for each stage, from foundation to roofing. The tracker shows what each stage should include.

2

Request proof on WhatsApp

Copy a ready-made message asking for the exact photos, video and receipts for that stage. It's polite, clear and the same every time.

3

Release the money, or hold it

Tick off the proof as it arrives. The next payment unlocks only when the checklist is complete, and the dashboard flags any unexplained gap.

Questions

Before you pre-order

Does my builder need to install anything?

No. Your builder keeps using WhatsApp. You do the tracking in the spreadsheet on your phone or laptop.

Is this a construction or inspection company?

No. Stage-Pay is a tool and a set of checklists you use yourself. For a large project, we also explain when it's worth paying an independent inspector, and what to ask them.

I've already started building. Is it too late?

No. Enter what you've sent so far and start using the proof checklists from your current stage.

What do I need to use it?

A free Google account for Google Sheets, or Microsoft Excel. It works on a phone, but it's easier to set up on a laptop.

When will I get it?

Within 4 weeks of your pre-order. If it isn't delivered by then, you get a full refund.
