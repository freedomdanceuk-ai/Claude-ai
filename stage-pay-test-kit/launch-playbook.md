# Stage-Pay: 7-Day Demand Test Playbook

**Goal:** Find out in one week whether Zimbabweans abroad will pay for Stage-Pay **before** we build the full product.

**Decision rule (decide now, before you look at results):**

| Result after 7 days | What it means | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| **10+ paid pre-orders** | Real demand | Build the full kit and deliver within 4 weeks |
| **3–9 pre-orders**, or 30+ WhatsApp "STAGE" sign-ups | Interest, but the offer isn't landing yet | Change the headline, price or format and test for one more week |
| **0–2 pre-orders** and few replies | Weak demand | Stop and refund anyone who paid. Go back to the other ideas. |

Comments and likes are not demand. Only payments and sign-ups count.

## Day 0: Setup (about 1–2 hours)

1. **Create a pre-order product on Selar** (selar.com). Selar accepts cards from abroad and pays out in Africa. Gumroad or Payhip also work.
   - Name: *Stage-Pay: Build at Home from Abroad (Founding Pre-order)*
   - Price: **$15**
   - Use the listing text in the "Selar listing" section below.
   - Upload `images/social-square-1080.png` as the product image.
2. **Set up WhatsApp Business** on a number you'll use for this. Add a short business description and an away message.
3. **Put your real links in the sales page.** Open `sales-page-standalone.html`, find the two lines near the bottom marked `Settings`, and fill in:
   - `PREORDER_URL` = your Selar product link
   - `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` = your WhatsApp Business number
4. **Put the page online.** Pick one:
   - Upload `sales-page-standalone.html` to a free host such as Netlify Drop (drag and drop the file) or GitHub Pages.
   - Or skip the page and send people straight to the Selar listing. That's the quickest option.
5. **Make a tracking sheet** with these columns: Date · Where posted · Link · Views/reach · Comments · DMs · STAGE sign-ups · Pre-orders.

> **Keep it honest.** Don't post fake testimonials, invented buyer numbers or statistics you can't source. Deliver within 4 weeks or refund every buyer, as the page promises. Your reputation in diaspora groups is your main asset.

## Where to post

Search Facebook for groups such as these, then join 8–12 active ones:
- "Zimbabweans in the UK", "Zimbabweans in Australia", "Zimbabweans in South Africa", "Zimbabweans in USA/Canada"
- Groups about building, stands and property in Zimbabwe (search: *stands Harare*, *building in Zimbabwe*, *Zim diaspora property*)

**Read each group's rules first.** Many ban sales posts. In those groups, use only **Post A** (the question post) and talk to people in comments and DMs. Never spam the same post in every group on the same day.

Also post on TikTok and Instagram Reels, WhatsApp Status, and in your own WhatsApp groups.

## Day 1–2: Post A, the question post (no link)

The goal is to start conversations and learn the exact words people use. It works in groups that ban selling.

> **Question for everyone building back home while living abroad 🏠**
>
> What has been the hardest part of managing your build from a distance?
>
> • Not knowing where the money actually went
> • Builders disappearing or going quiet
> • Family managing the site and it getting awkward
> • Costs going way over budget
> • Something else?
>
> I'm putting together a simple system to help with this, so I'd really like to hear real experiences. Comment or DM me.

**When people reply:** thank them, ask one follow-up question, and offer to DM them early access. Save their exact words. They become your future headlines.

## TikTok and Reels scripts (20–30 seconds each)

You can film these yourself talking to the camera, or record a voiceover with text on screen and images of the tracker.

**Script 1: "The blurry photo"**
- *(Hook, on-screen text: "You sent $3,000 for the roof…")*
- "You sent money for roofing. You got back one blurry photo of some timber. Is that the roof, or last month's photo?"
- "Here's the rule I'd use if I were building at home from abroad: **no proof, no payment.**"
- "Before each stage gets paid, ask for four things: photos of every wall from outside, a video walk-round, the specific work for that stage, and the receipts."
- "I made a tracker that does this for you. It's in my bio."

**Script 2: "Sent vs built"**
- *(Hook: "Do you actually know how much of your money is in the walls?")*
- "Most people building from abroad know how much they've sent. Very few know how much is actually built."
- *(Show the tracker image)* "This shows both. $14,200 sent, $11,600 backed by proof, and $2,600 flagged. That's a conversation you should have **before** the next payment, not after."
- "Founding price is $15. Link in bio."

**Script 3: "Family"** (for comments and stitches)
- "Asking your brother for receipts feels like accusing him. So don't make it personal. Make it the rule: 'I pay every stage the same way, with photos, video and receipts first.' Same rule for everyone, and nobody gets offended."

## 5 questions to ask anyone who replies

Talk to at least 5 people. Their answers shape the real product.

1. Where are you building, and what stage is it at now?
2. Who manages the site day to day: a builder, a relative or you?
3. How do you get updates now, and how often?
4. Have you ever paid for something that wasn't done, or wasn't done properly? What happened?
5. If a tool fixed this, what would it absolutely need to do? Would you pay $15–30 for it?
