Filming tips

  • Film in batches 2 days a week, for example Monday and Thursday.
  • Natural light, vertical video, 15–30 seconds.
  • No heavy filters. Customers worry photos won't match.
  • At every delivery, take one photo and one short video.
  • Post at 6–8pm, then check TikTok analytics after 2 weeks and adjust.
