## 1. Positioning: why you, not the shop next door

The Zimbabwe market is split between expensive big stores (TV Sales & Home, Bradlows-style chains, Gelmart) and informal makers with no trust or after-sales service. **Own the middle:** better prices than the chains, more trust and better finish than the roadside makers.

Your promise, repeated in every ad and video:

> **"Showroom quality. Factory prices. Delivered to your door. Pay in parts."**

Things you can offer that Zimbabwean buyers care about most:

| Buyer fear | Your answer (say it out loud in content) |
|---|---|
| "Is this a scam?" | Show the workshop, the team, real deliveries, customer faces. Pay-on-delivery deposit option. |
| "It will break in 6 months" | Show what's inside: frame wood, foam density, springs. Give a written **12-month frame warranty**. |
| "I can't pay all at once" | **Lay-by:** 30–50% deposit, balance in 1–3 months, deliver when paid. |
| "Will it fit my house?" | Exact measurements on every post. Offer **custom sizes and colours**. |
| "Delivery is a hassle" | Free delivery in Harare (or a flat fee), plus a courier option to Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo and other towns. |

## 2. The money machine: Click-to-WhatsApp ads

Your sales close on WhatsApp, so **every ad should open a WhatsApp chat**, not a profile visit.

### Facebook / Instagram (Meta Ads Manager)
- **Objective:** Engagement → Messaging apps → WhatsApp. (Or Sales → WhatsApp.)
- **Locations:** Harare, Chitungwiza, Ruwa, Norton, Bulawayo and other towns you deliver to. **Run a separate campaign targeting Zimbabweans in the UK, South Africa, USA, Australia and Botswana** (the diaspora section below explains why).
- **Age:** 24–55. Leave interests broad and let the video do the targeting.
- **Budget:** start with **$5–$10/day per ad set** and run 3–5 different creatives. After 3–4 days, turn off anything costing more than ~$1.50 per conversation. Double the budget on the winners every 3 days.
- **Pre-filled message:** "Hi, I'm interested in the [Product] I saw on your ad. Price and delivery please?" This tells you exactly which ad sold.

### TikTok
- Post **2–3 organic videos a day** (TikTok is free reach, so use it hard).
- When a video gets unusual views or comments, **Promote it** in the TikTok app with a goal of more messages or website visits (your wa.me link), at $5–$10 for 2–3 days. Only boost winners. Never boost a flop.
- If you can open a **TikTok Ads Manager** account, run Spark Ads (boosting your own organic posts) pointed to WhatsApp.

### Ad creatives that sell furniture in Zimbabwe
1. **Delivery video:** the furniture coming off the truck, into a real house, the customer smiling. ("Another one delivered to Borrowdale/Budiriro 🚚")
2. **Before & after:** an empty lounge, then the same room with your lounge suite.
3. **"What's inside":** cut open a cushion or show the frame. Proves quality.
4. **Price reveal:** "Guess the price of this 7-seater 👀" … reveal. Gets huge comments.
5. **Workshop / process:** timber → frame → foam → fabric → finished. Builds trust and shows you're a real maker.
6. **Customer testimonial:** 15 seconds, in Shona/Ndebele/English: "I paid lay-by over 2 months, it was delivered on time."

## 4. The diaspora goldmine 🇬🇧🇿🇦🇺🇸

Millions of Zimbabweans abroad send money home and **buy furniture for parents, new houses and family**. They have hard currency, they buy bigger sets, and they need someone they can trust.

- Run Facebook/TikTok ads targeting **UK, South Africa, USA, Australia, Botswana, Ireland, NZ**.
- Messaging: "Furnish your parents' home in Zimbabwe, delivered, with photo & video proof."
- Offer: **delivery video sent to the buyer** (furniture arriving, placed in the room, family saying thank you). This is your best marketing content too. Ask permission and post it.
- Accept payments diaspora buyers can make easily: Mukuru, WorldRemit, bank transfer, InnBucks, or pay via a family member locally.
- Packages: **"Mum's Bedroom Package"**, **"New House Starter Pack"** (lounge suite + dining + bed), **"Christmas Surprise"**.
- Peak timing: **November–December** (Christmas, people going home) and before **Mother's Day / Father's Day**.

## 5. Offers that make people buy now

Rotate these. Never discount everything all the time.

1. **Lay-by plan:** 30–50% deposit, pay over 1–3 months. This is huge in Zimbabwe; mention it in every post.
2. **Bundle deals:** Lounge suite + coffee table + TV stand = save $XX.
3. **Free delivery within Harare** (or "Free delivery this weekend only").
4. **Referral reward:** "Send us a friend who buys = $10–$20 cash back or free cushions." Give every customer 3 referral cards and a WhatsApp message to forward.
5. **Payday push:** run your strongest offers **25th–5th of the month** when salaries land.
6. **Scarcity:** "Only 4 sets left in this fabric", but only when true.
7. **Trade-in:** "Bring your old sofa, get $XX off." Creates great before/after content.

## 6. WhatsApp sales script (close more chats)

Most furniture sales are lost in slow or weak replies. **Reply within 5 minutes during business hours.**

1. **Greet + qualify:** "Hi 👋 thanks for reaching out! Which area are you in, and is this for lounge, bedroom or dining?"
2. **Send:** 2–3 photos + a short video + price + dimensions + colours.
3. **Handle the price question with value:** "This is $XXX, solid pine frame, high-density foam, 12-month warranty, free delivery in Harare. You can also do lay-by from $XXX deposit."
4. **Ask for the close:** "Should I reserve one for you? We only have X in this colour."
5. **Payment details:** EcoCash/InnBucks/bank/cash. Send a written receipt with your logo.
6. **Follow up** anyone who didn't buy: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 ("Still interested? We have a weekend deal…").
7. **After delivery:** ask for a photo/video review + referral. Add them to a **WhatsApp Broadcast list** or **Channel** for new stock alerts.

Build a **WhatsApp Channel** (e.g. "[Brand] New Stock & Deals") and invite every customer and lead. Post new arrivals and deals 3–4 times a week. Repeat customers are your cheapest sales.

## 7. Win offline too (where competitors aren't looking)

- **Estate agents & landlords:** offer furnishing packages for rentals and Airbnbs. Pay agents a referral commission.
- **New housing developments** (Harare and Bulawayo suburbs where people are building): flyers, a stand at site launches, partnerships with builders. New houses need complete furniture.
- **Lodges, guesthouses, offices, schools, churches:** bulk orders.
- **Wedding season / kitchen parties / "roora" season:** market bedroom suites and kitchen units as gifts.
- **Branded delivery vehicle** with your WhatsApp number = a moving billboard.
- **Showroom or display corner,** even a small one. Being able to see the shop builds trust online.
